Triggerbot

Triggerbot automatically fires when the crosshair is on a target.

Enabled

Master switch for the Triggerbot feature.

First Slow

Slows down while firing to mimic the aim slowdown caused by holding the left mouse button.

Hit Chance

Calculates hit chance from weapon spread and the target hitbox; only fires when the hit chance is above the set value.

Interval Delay

Controls the delay between burst shots.

Distance

Filters out targets beyond the specified distance.

Hitbox

The target hitboxes allowed to be fired upon.
