Misc

Auxiliary features related to movement enhancements and world rendering.

Movement

Bunnyhop

Automatically jumps on landing.

Fast Ladder

Climbs ladders quickly with a fixed pitch and yaw posture.

Viewmodel

Enables first-person weapon model adjustments, including the following options:

  • Viewmodel Enabled: Enables first-person weapon model adjustments.
  • Sway: Enables weapon sway.
  • Sway Scale: Weapon sway amplitude.
  • Viewmodel Forward: Forward offset of the weapon model.
  • Viewmodel Right: Right offset of the weapon model.
  • Viewmodel Up: Up offset of the weapon model.

World

No Fog

Disables fog.

No Fog Sky

Disables sky fog.

No Vomit

Disables vomit and similar stain effects.

Flash Boost

Enhances flashlight brightness and range.

FullBright

Removes scene lighting and shadows; when disabled, models may remain highlighted.

Anti Shake

Removes screen shake from Tank hazards and explosions that do not affect bullet trajectory.
