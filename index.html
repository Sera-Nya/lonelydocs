Welcome to Lonely Cheat

A "Left 4 Dead 2" cheat with a modern interface and an excellent user experience.​

Introduction

Designed from the start with a focus on good experience and stealth, it delivers a polished and convenient UI along with features such as Weapon Group, Delay Shoot, and Humanized.

Usage

Launch Left 4 Dead 2, then start Lonely Cheat. Open the in-game menu to enable or configure each feature. Categories are listed in the sidebar; expand a category to view its options. Settings you change are applied immediately.
